Tour With Joel / Reading & Watching / Ireland

Music, memory, rebellion and Atlantic weather.

Stories, films and music connected to the places and atmosphere that tend to stay with people long after travelling through Ireland.

Ireland often deepens afterwards.

You’ve heard a lot on tour. This is a short list of the books, films and music that tend to help it land — the ones I’d genuinely point you towards on the coach home.

Reading

Books

  • 📚 Say Nothing — Patrick Radden Keefe

    If Belfast or Derry got under your skin, read this next. It follows one woman's disappearance during the Troubles and the lives it fractured. Gets closer to what that period actually felt like than any history book I know — and it reads like a thriller.

  • 📚 The Immortal Irishman — Timothy Egan

    Rebel, transported to Tasmania, escaped, became a Civil War general in America. Thomas Francis Meagher's life runs through the Famine, Irish revolution and the diaspora in one extraordinary arc. If your family has Irish roots, this one will hit harder than you expect.

  • 📚 Brooklyn — Colm Tóibín

    A young woman leaves Ireland for New York in the 1950s and finds herself caught between two lives. Quiet and understated, but it carries everything we talked about — emigration, longing, the pull of home. One of the best Irish novels of recent years.

Watching

Films & TV

  • 🎬 Michael Collins (1996)

    If the 1916 Rising, the War of Independence and partition felt like a lot to absorb on tour, this puts it all together. Liam Neeson, big production, genuinely gripping — and by the end the whole arc makes sense.

  • 📺 Derry Girls

    Funny, chaotic and unexpectedly moving. Set in Derry during the Troubles, it follows a group of teenagers completely absorbed in ordinary life while the city is under pressure around them. The best way I know to make Northern Ireland feel human rather than heavy.

  • 🎬 Brooklyn (2015)

    Saoirse Ronan, Ireland to New York. Beautifully made and emotionally true — the emigration story you heard about on tour, told through one face.

Listening

Music

  • 🎵Fields of Athenry

    The Famine, emigration and quiet defiance in three minutes. You've probably heard it already — it sounds a little different now.

  • 🎵The Parting Glass

    A song for farewells and the strange feeling of leaving somewhere before you're ready to.

  • 🎵Raglan Road

    Patrick Kavanagh's poetry set to an old Irish tune. One of the great Irish songs for carrying a place home with you.
